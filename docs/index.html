Potato price forecast for Assam

A working prototype for NESFIC 2026, challenge D-35 (Agricultural Commodity Price Intelligence and Forecasting). Pick a district. The forecast is worked out in your browser from public market prices.

Prototype, not advice. In our tests this model does about as well as the simplest method, which is to assume the price stays where it is. It does not yet beat it. See the full test results.
