Crop price outlook for Assam

A prototype built for NESFIC 2026, challenge D-35. It reads public market prices and estimates where the wholesale price of a crop in a district is heading. Everything is worked out here in your browser.

Look ahead

See what it said in any past week, and what happened next.

Prototype, not advice. Tested against past prices, this model does not reliably beat the simplest method, which is to assume the price stays where it is. The track record below shows how it did for the crop and district you pick. Read the full test results for potato.
